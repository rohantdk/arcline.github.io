About

One person, from first scope to launch day.

I'm a freelance web developer and AI engineer based in Nashik, India. I build websites, Shopify stores and AI tools that work on launch day and that clients can run themselves afterwards.

Most of my clients are manufacturers, exporters and small brands across Maharashtra. I also work internationally: I built an AI risk-assessment tool for a B2B advisory firm in Germany. I work solo under the studio name Arcline.

What I do

Six kinds of work, one standard.

  • Websites

    Fast, hand-coded HTML, CSS and JavaScript sites with enquiry capture, SEO basics and hosting set up.

  • Shopify stores

    Theme customisation in Liquid, with Razorpay, GST, shipping zones and launch handled.

  • AI engineering

    FastAPI backends on Gemini, Claude or local models: structured outputs, retrieval and speech-to-text.

  • Mobile apps

    Android apps in Expo and React Native, with Node and MongoDB backends.

  • Automation

    Lead pipelines and back-office flows with Make.com, Google Sheets, Brevo and Supabase.

  • Design and content

    Brand identity, packaging artwork, Figma screens, landing-page copy and LinkedIn carousels.
